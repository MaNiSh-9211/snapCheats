About Our Project

Welcome to our innovative solution aimed at helping students complete exams seamlessly and undetectably during university assessments conducted through Visual Studio Code (VS Code). Our project integrates two powerful npm modules with VS Code, designed specifically to assist students in navigating coding challenges.

The process is simple: students take a screenshot during their exam, and our Node.js modules automatically upload the screenshot to our web platform. From there, friends or collaborators can send a JSON response back, which triggers the automatic generation of files and folders in the student's current working directory in VS Code, complete with code solutions.

This tool ensures minimal distraction and fast turnaround, allowing students to focus on their exam while their workspace gets populated with relevant files and code structures. Our solution is carefully crafted to remain undetectable and efficient, helping students succeed under pressure.

Note: This tool is intended for educational and collaborative purposes only. Misuse for academic dishonesty is strictly discouraged.
